This Privacy Policy describes how FleetHub (“FleetHub”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, discloses and retains information in connection with the FleetHub fleet-management application and its related interfaces (the “Service”).

The Service is a business tool licensed to organizations (each, a “Customer”). Individuals who use the Service do so as authorized personnel of a Customer (each, an “Operator”). The Customer determines the purposes for which Operator and vehicle records are processed; FleetHub processes them on the Customer’s behalf and in accordance with this Policy.

1 Scope

This Policy applies to information processed through the Service. It does not apply to any Customer’s own systems, nor to third-party services that a Customer may operate independently of FleetHub.

Access to the Service is by invitation only. There is no public registration, and the Service is not directed to consumers or to individuals under the age of eighteen.

2 Information We Collect

2.1 Operator account information

When a Customer’s administrator invites an Operator, and when that Operator completes registration, we collect:

  • Full name, used to attribute recorded activity to the person who performed it;
  • Email address, used for authentication, account recovery and delivery of verification codes;
  • Mobile telephone number, used solely for the delivery of one-time verification codes;
  • Language preference, role and suspension status, used to render the interface and enforce permissions.

Passwords are not stored. We retain only a one-way cryptographic derivation (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, per-account salt, in PHC string format) from which the original password cannot be recovered.

One-time verification codes are likewise not stored in plain text. Codes issued by FleetHub are retained only as a cryptographic derivation computed together with the recipient’s address, such that neither the code nor its recipient can be read back from our records.

2.2 Operational records

In the course of ordinary use, the Service records:

  • Vehicle records — vehicle identification number, make, model, model year, trim, body style, engine and status;
  • Position reports — latitude, longitude, reported accuracy, timestamp, and the Operator who submitted the report;
  • Activity events — status changes, location assignments and attachments, each with a timestamp and the Operator responsible;
  • Photographs — images captured or selected by an Operator in connection with a vehicle;
  • Dismissed alerts — the condition dismissed, its description, the Operator and the time.

Position reports are collected only when an Operator affirmatively submits one. The Service does not perform background or continuous location tracking.

2.3 Photographic metadata

Image files commonly embed metadata describing the location, device and settings under which they were captured. Before any image is written to storage, FleetHub removes EXIF, XMP and comment metadata from JPEG, PNG and WebP files. Files are additionally validated against declared type and rejected if they exceed 8 MiB, 12,000 pixels on any side, or 40 megapixels. HEIC and HEIF files are not accepted, as their metadata cannot be reliably removed without decoding.

2.4 Server logs

Our servers emit one structured log entry per request, recording the HTTP method, path, response status and elapsed time. Request bodies, headers, authentication tokens and account identifiers are not written to logs. Email addresses appearing in diagnostic context are reduced to their domain.

2.5 Information we do not collect

  • We do not use analytics platforms, advertising identifiers or third-party tracking cookies.
  • We do not collect payment card information through the Service.
  • We do not purchase or otherwise acquire personal information from data brokers.

3 Text Messaging

Mobile telephone numbers are used exclusively to deliver one-time verification codes. FleetHub does not send marketing, promotional or informational messages of any kind.

Mobile opt-in data and telephone numbers are never sold, rented, leased or shared with third parties for any purpose, other than disclosure to the messaging provider strictly necessary to transmit the message to the recipient.

  • Consent. An Operator enters their own mobile number on the sign-in screen and requests a code. A message is transmitted only in direct response to that request.
  • Frequency. One message per sign-in or account-recovery attempt initiated by the Operator.
  • Opt-out. Reply STOP to any message to cease delivery. Reply HELP for assistance.
  • Cost. Message and data rates may apply according to the recipient’s mobile plan.

Where verification codes are delivered by SMS, the code is generated and validated by our messaging provider rather than by FleetHub. FleetHub records that a verification was requested and subsequently completed, together with the account and time, but does not receive or retain the code itself.

4 How We Use Information

  • To authenticate Operators and maintain sessions;
  • To deliver one-time verification codes by email or text message;
  • To provide the fleet-management functions the Customer has licensed;
  • To attribute recorded activity to the Operator who performed it, so that the Customer retains an accurate operational record;
  • To enforce access controls, apply rate limits and investigate suspected abuse;
  • To maintain, secure and troubleshoot the Service.

We do not use Operator or vehicle information to train machine-learning models, to build advertising profiles, or for any purpose unrelated to providing the Service.

6 Segregation of Customer Data

Every record in the Service is associated with the Customer to which it belongs, and every read and write operation is constrained to that Customer. No Customer can access, modify or infer the existence of another Customer’s records.

Within a Customer, an Operator’s visibility is further limited to the vehicles at the active locations assigned to them, and their permitted actions are determined by their assigned role. A suspended account retains no access.

Where FleetHub support access is required, it is granted only by the Customer, by issuing a support key. Such access is time-limited to eight hours, is recorded, and may be terminated at any time by either the Customer or the support account.

7 Retention

Records are retained for as long as the Customer’s account remains open. The Service does not delete records on the basis of age. No automatic expiry schedule applies to account information, vehicle records, locations, position reports, activity events, photographs or dismissed alerts.

Credentials are the exception, and they expire by time rather than by removal. A session ceases to be valid twelve hours after it is issued; a verification code ceases to be valid within minutes of being sent, once used, or once its permitted attempts are exhausted. An expired credential is refused on every subsequent use. The corresponding record is retained until the Customer’s account is deleted.

A Customer may delete an individual account, or the Customer’s entire account, at any time. Deletion is immediate and permanent, and removes the associated records together with any stored photograph files. See section 9.

8 Security

  • All traffic is served over HTTPS with HTTP Strict Transport Security.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted, iterated one-way derivations.
  • Sessions expire twelve hours after issuance, are invalidated on sign-out, and all other sessions are invalidated when a password is changed.
  • Verification codes expire, permit a limited number of attempts, and are invalidated once used or if delivery fails.
  • Requests for verification codes, password attempts and account creation are rate limited.
  • Error responses returned to clients do not disclose internal system detail.

No method of transmission or storage is entirely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9 Your Choices and Rights

Because Operators use the Service under the authority of a Customer, requests concerning an Operator’s information should ordinarily be directed to that Customer’s administrator, who can:

  • Correct an Operator’s name, email address, telephone number or role;
  • Suspend an Operator’s access;
  • Delete an Operator’s account, which removes their name, email address, telephone number, location assignments and support access records;
  • Delete the Customer’s entire account, which removes all associated Operators, vehicles, locations, position reports, events, photographs, alerts, keys and sessions.

Following deletion of an Operator account, historical activity records retain only the pseudonymous identifier of the account that performed each action. That identifier no longer resolves to any individual, and is retained so that the Customer’s operational history remains complete.

You may also contact us directly at the address above, and we will respond consistent with applicable law and with our obligations to the relevant Customer.
